Family-friendly places everyone will love

Spots the whole family can enjoy together — plus a fun fact about each one.

Lekki Conservation Centre, Lagos

A canopy walkway and nature trail the kids will talk about for weeks.

Fun fact: it holds one of the longest canopy walkways in Africa.

Nike Art Gallery, Lagos

Five floors of Nigerian art — a fun, free way to spend an afternoon together.

Fun fact: it displays over 8,000 pieces of art, one of West Africa's largest private collections.

Yankari Game Reserve, Bauchi

Warm springs, a safari drive and elephants — a proper family adventure.

Fun fact: it's home to Nigeria's largest surviving elephant population.

Millennium Park, Abuja

Open lawns, walking paths and a lake — easy, relaxed fun for every age.

Fun fact: it was designed by an Israeli architect to mark Nigeria's new millennium in 2003.
